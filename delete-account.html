Delete your Telos account

Telos by Mohd Hussain Ahmed

You can delete your Telos account and its data at any time. Choose either option below. You don't need the app installed for option 2.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open Telos.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: By email

If you no longer have the app, or can't sign in:

  1. Email telosapp3@gmail.com from the email address you use with Telos.
  2. Use the subject Delete my Telos account.

We will confirm the request came from the account owner, delete the account and reply to confirm, within 30 days and usually much sooner. If you email from a different address, we may ask you to verify that you own the account first.

What gets deleted

From our servers, permanently:

  • your email address
  • your name (if you signed in with Google)
  • your account ID and sign-in method
  • your sign-in sessions, which signs you out everywhere

This is all the personal data Telos keeps on its servers. We remove it from our authentication system right away. Copies in our hosting provider's routine backups are removed as those backups expire, within 30 days. Our provider may keep minimal security logs, such as sign-in timestamps and IP addresses, for a limited time to protect against abuse.

Data on your phone

Your journal, plans, notes, sessions and browsing data are stored only on your phone and never reach our servers. Deleting your account does not remove them from your phone. To erase them:

  • clear Telos's storage in Android Settings → Apps → Telos → Storage → Clear storage, or
  • uninstall Telos.

If Android backup is on, a copy of this app data may also be in your Google account's device backup. You can manage it in your Google account settings.

Questions

Email telosapp3@gmail.com. The Privacy Policy explains in full what Telos collects and why.
