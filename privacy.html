Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Telos Android app ("Telos", "the app") handles, where it is kept and what control you have over it. Telos is developed by Mohd Hussain Ahmed, an individual developer based in India ("we", "us"). You can reach us at telosapp3@gmail.com.

In short

  • Your journal, plans, notes, sessions and browsing stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • Our servers hold only your account details: your email address, an account ID and, if you sign in with Google, your name and profile photo link.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not sell or share your data.
  • You can delete your account at any time, in the app or by email.

1. Data that stays on your device

Most of what you create in Telos is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • focus sessions, schedules and session lengths
  • reflections and intentions you write
  • plans, tasks and notes
  • in-app browser history, bookmarks, open tabs and your list of downloads
  • cookies and site data from websites you visit in the in-app browser
  • app settings and permission setup state

This data is not sent to us and we cannot see it. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include Telos's app data in your device backup, which Google stores in your Google account under Google's Privacy Policy. Some internal app state is excluded from that backup.

You can clear your browsing history and downloads list in the app under Browse → Library. To erase everything Telos stores on your phone, clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Telos → Storage, or uninstall the app.

Files you download in the in-app browser are saved to your phone's shared Downloads folder, like downloads from any other browser. They are ordinary files that belong to you. Clearing Telos's storage or uninstalling Telos does not delete them. You can manage them in your phone's Files app.

Telos asks you to sign in with Google or with a one-time code sent to your email address. When you sign in, we receive and store:

3. Service providers

We use these providers to run the account system. They process data on our behalf, under their own security and privacy terms:

  • Supabase hosts authentication and stores your account data on servers in Supabase's Asia region. It also sends the one-time sign-in codes by email. See the Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Google handles "Continue with Google" sign-in and shares your name, email address and profile photo link with us when you choose it. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Because these providers run infrastructure in several countries, your account data may be processed outside India. We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone else, except where the law requires us to.

4. The in-app browser

Telos includes a web browser. When you visit a website, your phone connects to that site directly. The site receives your request, including your IP address, and may set cookies, as any browser would. Websites you visit are governed by their own privacy policies. We do not receive or monitor what you browse. Your history, bookmarks and tabs stay on your phone (see section 1).

5. Permissions and the Accessibility service

Telos asks for some Android permissions to run focus sessions. None of them send data to us.

PermissionWhat it's used for
NotificationsShowing the running session timer and the break after a session ends
Alarms and reminders (exact alarms)Starting scheduled sessions and ending them on time
Foreground service and battery-optimisation exemptionKeeping the session timer accurate while Telos is in the background
Do Not Disturb accessTurning the screen greyscale during a session using Android's built-in display effects
Accessibility service (Samsung phones only, optional)Switching Android's Colour correction to greyscale when a session starts and back when it ends

The Accessibility service is used only on Samsung phones, where Android's standard greyscale effect doesn't work. It is off until you turn it on, and Telos explains why before you are taken to Android Settings. The service:

  • does not read or collect anything on your screen (it is configured so it cannot access window content)
  • does not see which apps you open (it is configured to receive no accessibility events)
  • does not collect, store or share any personal information

It only reads your phone's Colour correction setting and triggers the accessibility shortcut that turns it on or off. You can turn it off at any time in Android Settings → Accessibility → Installed apps → Telos.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we remove it from our authentication system immediately. Any copies in our provider's routine backups are removed as those backups expire, within 30 days. Data on your phone stays until you clear it or uninstall the app. See Delete your account.

7. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other laws that may apply to you, you can:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and receive a summary of it
  • ask us to correct or update it
  • ask us to erase it, or delete your account yourself in the app
  • withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become incapacitated
  • raise a grievance with us, and if it is not resolved, complain to the Data Protection Board of India

To make a request, email telosapp3@gmail.com from the address linked to your account. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your sign-in session is kept in Android's encrypted secure storage. Account deletion is handled on our server and only ever acts on the account that is signed in. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect, which limits what could ever be exposed.

9. Children

Telos is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how Telos handles data, for example when we add new features, we will update this page and its effective date. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
