Telos

An Android app that helps you put your phone down at night, clear your mind and settle into sleep.

Telos doesn't rely on hard blockers. You set a wind-down session, use the phone for things that help you finish the day (a contained browser, a short reflection, a plan for tomorrow), and the screen goes greyscale so the rest of the phone is less tempting. When the session ends, you put the phone down.

Your data

Your journal, plans, notes and browsing stay on your phone. Our servers hold only your sign-in details. There are no ads and no analytics. The Privacy Policy has the details.
