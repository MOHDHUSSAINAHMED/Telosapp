Telos

A focus app for Android that gives your screen time an ending.

Your phone never really tells you when to stop. A quick check turns into an hour of scrolling. Telos gives every session a clear stopping point.

  • Intentional limits. Choose exactly how long you want to be on your phone, then start a session.
  • Greyscale focus. Outside Telos, your whole phone turns grey, so other apps lose their pull.
  • A built-in space. Inside Telos, everything stays in colour, with tools to browse, plan and reflect.
  • A clear ending. When time is up, the session ends and a short break helps you step back into your day.

Your data

Your journal, plans, notes and browsing stay on your phone. Our servers hold only your sign-in details. There are no ads and no analytics. The Privacy Policy has the details.
